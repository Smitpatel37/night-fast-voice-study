📋 Participant Consent Form
Please read the following consent form carefully and confirm your agreement before proceeding.

1. Purpose of Study

This voice collection session is part of the NIGHT-FAST research project at the University Medical Center Göttingen (UMG). The purpose is to evaluate the accuracy of a voice-controlled interface for medical drone dispatch. Your voice recordings will be used exclusively for academic research and thesis evaluation.

2. What You Will Do

You will be presented with scenario descriptions related to medical drone operations. For each scenario, you will speak a natural voice command in either German or English. Your voice will be recorded via your device's microphone. Each scenario takes approximately 10–30 seconds.

3. Data Collected

We will record:

  • Your voice recordings (WAV format, 16 kHz mono)
  • An automatically assigned anonymous participant identifier (no real name required)
  • The scenario prompts and metadata (timestamp, language, browser)

4. Data Protection (GDPR Art. 6, 13, 14)

All data is stored locally on your device and exported as a ZIP file. No data is transmitted to any server during the recording process. You may delete your recordings at any time. Your participant ID is automatically assigned and contains no personally identifiable information.

5. Voluntary Participation

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time without consequence. You may skip any scenario you are uncomfortable with. You may re-record any response as many times as you wish.

6. Contact

For questions about this study, contact the NIGHT-FAST research team at UMG.
